# Food Ordering System – Canteen
## Problem Statement

College canteens often rely on manual processes for handling food orders, which can result in long queues, order-processing delays, incorrect billing, and difficulty maintaining customer and order records. Students may also find it inconvenient to browse available food items and manage their orders efficiently. At the same time, canteen administrators need a structured way to manage the menu, customer information, orders, and order status.

The **Food Ordering System – Canteen** addresses these challenges by providing a Java-based console application that organizes the food-ordering process. It allows customers to browse the menu, manage their cart, place orders, and view their order history, while providing administrators with tools to manage the canteen's operations.

## Scope of the Project

This project provides a standalone, console-based Java application for managing food ordering and basic canteen operations. The scope includes:

- Customer registration, login, and account management
- Browsing and searching available food items
- Categorization and management of canteen food items
- Adding, removing, and updating items in the shopping cart
- Order placement and checkout with automatic bill calculation
- Application of applicable discounts and taxes
- Maintaining customer and order records using file-based storage
- Viewing previous orders and managing order status
- Administrative functionality for managing food items, customers, and orders
- Input validation and exception handling for reliable system operation

## Target Users

- **Students/Customers:** Users who want to browse the canteen menu, select food items, place orders, and view their order history
- **Canteen Administrators:** Users responsible for managing food items, customer records, orders, and order status
- **Canteen Staff:** Users who need to process and update customer orders efficiently

## High-Level Features

1. **User Registration & Authentication**  
   Provides registration and login functionality for customers and administrators.

2. **Menu Management**  
   Allows users to view and search available food items, while administrators can add, update, or remove menu items.

3. **Shopping Cart**  
   Enables customers to add food items to their cart, modify quantities, and remove unwanted items before checkout.

4. **Order Processing**  
   Allows customers to confirm their cart and place food orders while generating the corresponding order details.

5. **Billing & Calculation**  
   Automatically calculates the order amount, including applicable discounts and taxes.

6. **Order History**  
   Maintains previous order information so customers can review their past orders.

7. **Order Status Management**  
   Allows administrators or staff to update the status of customer orders.

8. **File-Based Data Storage**  
   Stores important application data such as users, food items, and orders using files, allowing information to persist between program executions.

9. **Input Validation & Exception Handling**  
   Handles invalid user inputs and runtime errors to ensure smoother and more reliable program execution.
